## What changes for the user

- **One theme** on every page: dark navy, neon-blue highlights, the tab bar as pills.
- **Mission Control is the AI office:**
  - a left menu (counts, New Mission ⌘K, Dashboard · My Missions · Templates · Knowledge · Integrations · History,
    workspaces, the Usage card);
  - the office: one desk per agent in rings round a glowing globe (the Brain's seat), chibis in hoodies and
    headphones, step cards with timers and badges, sub-agents as smaller chibis, dotted lines to the globe;
  - **Flow · List · Calendar**;
  - a right column: Mission Progress, Current Task (checklist, Stop, Chat, Hand over, Answer), Recent Activity;
  - the composer: **Ask AI**, **Build a Mission**, **Use a Template**; Attach (10 files, 20 MB each, under
    `attachments/`), **Web ▾** (Web + Brave, Brave only, No web), Model, Account, Folder, Workspace; ⌘↵.
- **New pages:** My Missions (filters), History (results, files changed, search), Templates, Knowledge (Remove hides a
  skill from the agents you start next, never deletes it), Integrations, and the Calendar (scheduled templates' next
  runs and finished agents).
- **The particle Brain is gone** (blob.js, brainshape.js); the globe is the Brain's seat.
- **The README** has a new "## Mission Control (your AI office)" section.

## How it was built and checked

- **Branch** `new-look` in `../Ai_Browser-wt/look`, from main 8d7c46f. 17 tasks (Task 1, the optional usage probe, was
  skipped), each by a fresh implementer, each with a fresh spec-and-quality review and fix rounds where needed
  (Tasks 3, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 17 needed them).

  - **"No web" left a silent web route** (the skills' Docker shell): now blocked, with a second lock

    attachments; clearing them could follow a link out of the scratch folder. All fixed, each with tests (the
    reserved-name rule was fuzzed with ~590,000 names).

    desks; the heading covered far cards; the room shrank 30% for no reason. All fixed and measured in a browser.
  - **Other bugs:** a stopped answer leaked into the next result; the drawer's accounts went stale; template runs
    wiped attached files' chips; History's times wrapped; the Calendar ignored the workspace.
- **The final whole-branch review** (most capable model): safety rules and all five Review Focus areas hold. Two
  cross-task bugs (the checklist emptied by a long answer; the Calendar rebuilding on every agent update) and ten
  recommended items were fixed in one wave (F1–F12), re-reviewed, and the README's "No web" sentence corrected.
