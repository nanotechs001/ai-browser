# One shared browser connection (CDP relay): report

Date: 2026-10-03.

You asked: "Brave will still ask "Allow" for each agent and the bar will keep coming back. resolve this, it should only
ask once and display once". This is that fix, built from the cdp-relay spec
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-cdp-relay-design.md`) and its plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-cdp-relay.md`).

## What changes for you

- **One question per app run:** AI Browser now holds the only connection to your Brave. Every agent and every Claude Code
  session (while the app runs) goes through it. Brave asks "Allow remote debugging?" once when the app connects, and
  again only after Brave restarts. No question per agent, per New chat, per crash, per session.
- **The bar stays closed:** close Brave's "controlled by automated test software" bar and it stays closed while the app's
  one connection lives. It comes back only when AI Browser or Brave restarts. AI Browser never touches it.
- **Agents see only their own tabs:** each agent and session sees and drives only the tabs it opened, their popups, and
  tabs you handed it. Your other tabs' titles and addresses never reach them. Take back cuts at once.
- **A short list of commands:** reading cookies, closing Brave, changing permissions or downloads, overriding the user
  agent or `navigator.webdriver`: all refused before Brave sees them.
- **AI Home lists what is connected:** a new list under the browser card ("Using your Brave through AI Browser"), with
  Disconnect for each agent and Claude Code session.
- **Claude Code sessions:** while the app runs they go through it (no question); without the app they connect directly as
  before. If the app stops under a session, it says so and never connects to Brave by itself until you choose
  (`sandbox_start(chrome=true)`).

## How it was built and checked

- Test-first, nine tasks, one commit each, on branch `cdp-relay`.
- A fresh review of the plan before the build (on the most capable model): 1 critical, 4 important and 7 minor
  findings, all handled test-first (the critical one: a bot check found in Website QA's own sandbox would have been
  dropped and never reached AI Home).
- A fresh review of the whole branch after the build: no critical findings, 1 important (fixed), 4 minor (one re-graded
  important and fixed, three deferred below).
- Mutation checks on the key relay behaviours: each one, broken on purpose, makes its test fail.
- The browser tests also run through the relay in a throwaway Chrome (direct and relayed).
- Whole suite: before the branch 1080 passed / 9 skipped; after the build 1225 passed / 9 skipped; after the final
  fixes 1239 passed / 9 skipped (the skips are Windows-only tests).

- **Plan:** Ruling: Tokens.revoke keeps the revoked token as "gone", so a closed agent's engine gets 410 (the spec: "410: ... or its agent has closed") rather than a bare 403 — cost if wrong: none beyond a few bytes per closed agent.

## Your hand check (in your own Brave, after you restart the app)

Nobody has counted these in a real Brave yet; they rest on Chromium's source and a headless probe:

1. With the switch ticked, start `uv run ai-browser app`. Brave asks once: click Allow.
2. Give two tasks. Brave asks no more. AI Home lists AI Browser and the two agents.
3. Close the "controlled by automated test software" bar. Start a third task, and press New chat on one agent. The bar
   stays closed and nothing asks.
4. With the app running, ask a Claude Code session to open a page. Nothing asks; its tab joins the "Claude Code"
   group; AI Home lists it.
5. Hand a tab to an agent, then take it back. The agent's next step says it lost the tab.
6. Disconnect the session in AI Home. Its next browser call says so, and Brave doesn't ask.
7. Quit Brave and start it again. AI Home says closed. Give an agent a message: Brave asks once, click Allow, and the
   agent carries on.
8. Restart Brave once more, click Connect in AI Home, and click Deny. Nothing asks again until Try again or a new task.
9. Quit the app. The Claude Code session's next browser call says AI Browser stopped, and Brave doesn't ask.

Note how many times Brave asked in steps 1-4 and whether the bar came back in step 3. If either differs, the texts get
corrected before the next wave.
