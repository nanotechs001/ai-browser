Settings

AI Browser app

The shortcut shows and hides the floating chat from any app.

To set it up, run this in a terminal. It shows what it downloads, and asks first.

App window

Which browser draws AI Browser's window

The microphone is allowed per browser: after a switch, the window asks for it once more.

Accounts

Your Claude logins. New agents use the default one, and each agent keeps its own until you move it.

Permissions

The folders agents worked in: whether you trust each one, and what you allowed always there. A folder you trust loads its own CLAUDE.md, agents and settings.

Where agents browse

The Brave profile agents work in. AI Home lists its tabs, and only counts the tabs of your other profiles.

Brain

While this is on, AI Browser reads these sites in your open tabs in Brave, in the profile where agents browse, and the parts of pages you picked to watch. What a page says is treated as data, never as instructions. AI Browser never sends anything: a reply goes into the reply box only when you click Use draft.

What the Brain listens to

What you uncheck is never sent to the Brain. Gmail, Slack, Asana and watched page parts are read only while the switch above is on.

The Brain shows each hand-off with its exact words, and sends it after 15 seconds unless you press Cancel: only from your own words, and only to an agent that is working. What comes from an agent's result, an email, Slack, Asana or a watched page always waits for your Send, and a new agent for your Start. Off: every hand-off waits for your Send.
